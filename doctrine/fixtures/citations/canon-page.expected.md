# A canon page — the citation fixture (043)

Read as `canon/work/DOCTRINE.md`: every citation of a DOCTRINE-homed id below is a self
citation, and every other one crosses.

- **Naming law:** a self citation, bare — the home is the citing document, so the
  pointer points at where it stands and strips.
- The typed absence (birthplace: the arborist close-out) — a self citation leading a
  parenthetical: the id and its separator go, the rest keeps its bytes.
- A dropped baton — a pair of self citations joined by `/`: both go, and the
  parenthetical with them.
- The verdict law (architect.md, the verdict law) — a cross citation, bare: it reads the home the table names.
- The Sovereign's thinking partner (the sixth mantle — mentat.md) — a cross citation trailing a
  parenthetical: ` — ` joins the home to its neighbour.
- The words mean what [STANDARD.md](STANDARD.md) says — the home file is already
  named on this line, so the pointer is redundant and strips.
- Another building's register keeps its own numbers: belvedere:D11, simmy:D4.
- A form being SHOWN is not a citation: `[D19](DECISIONS.md)`, `D44`.
- A live entry keeps its id (D82) — nothing at or above D77 is dead.
- D28's law and the bare (D44) beside it: no shape consumes a possessive, so the whole
  line is left standing for the hand — both citations with it, all or nothing.
- A shape split by a hard wrap is a hand edit, so the tail of this sentence stands
  (D48).

inside one is still a citation — and a fence marker resets the inline-tick parity rather than
opening one span the width of the block:
