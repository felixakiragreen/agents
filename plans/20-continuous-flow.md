## What the sitting rules

   [belvedere/plans/flow-keel.md](../belvedere/plans/flow-keel.md): the flow as the
   batch note's successor (or its data twin), the landing grammar's `holds`, the
   engine's D10 posture, the Steward's gate. Belvedere builds the engine; canon rules
   the grammar and the mantle (flow-keel §7).
2. **The Dispatcher mantle's fate** — v0 usage is the measure: the flow chapter ran a
   real batch with the between-sessions logistics mechanized; what remains of the
   charter, and what retires.
3. **The D64 grammar asks, batched** (all filed with evidence, 2026-08-27/28, drained
   at GA-11):
   - `Baton.kind` (`move`/`wave`/`fork`) as a field — the rail splits shapes by regex
     today, and plurality with neither marker renders "shape unstated"
     ([b3 §§F4–F5](../belvedere/plans/b3-baton-rail.md)).
   - The fork's `recommendation` as a slot — scanned out of the sentence containing
     "recommend" today.
   - A row's `branch` as a slot — the naive doc-read matched 12 of 62 live work docs
     and every hit was retrospective; narrowed to header-block worktree lines: 0 of 62
     (b3 §F5). The worktree→fire composition needs it.
   - **Holder precedence:** `classifyBaton` reads the instrument before Felix, which
     inverted all three of the city's fireable batons on the live corpus (each said
     the fire was Felix's and conditional; the rail armed Dispatch buttons) — B3's
     warning-card mitigation stands until the grammar carries the holder
     ([b3 §E2](../belvedere/plans/b3-baton-rail.md)).
   - **The Dispatcher-holder case:** a Dispatcher-tended chain has no legal baton
     holder — Belvedere's own tail linted `ledger.baton` three entries running
     (B13/P5/B14); writing `fire <row>` would arm a button for work the batch note
     says is the Dispatcher's.
4. **Cross-building Depends-on** — no legal form exists for lunchbox O2 → theseus
   T12a (two boards, one register). Options preserved from 18d's filing: a qualified
   id (`theseus:T12a`), a gate row per crossing (D44 read literally), or a ruling
   that the column stays strictly local and cross-building sequencing is
   batch-note/flow territory. The flow's cross-board strings make this the natural
   sitting to rule it. (The same-building cross-DOCUMENT case was a tool scope bug —
   row 19 item 7.)
5. **Row 17's verdict, folded** — the storage experiment reads the flow keel as its
