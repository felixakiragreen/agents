  sovereign's yes to two different objects. The ask: may **bless** become the one
  spoken verb for the Felix-yes, countersign retiring from the working vocabulary
  while the D63 `✓ Felix` marker stands unchanged?

  park, true, fold, strike, pre-chew, cut, baton, Felix-gate, waggle) — the sitting
  extends or amends it, never forks it.

  deploys there for free.
