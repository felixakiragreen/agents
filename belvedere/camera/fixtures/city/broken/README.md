# broken — the lint control

**Parser-as-lint, on purpose.** This building's boards are malformed in six ways at once, so
`doctrine lint` goes **red** here and the deck's building page has to render the failures
with their verbatim excerpts instead of a blank panel. It is the control for alpha and beta:
a linter that reads them green and this one green is a linter that is not reading at all.

Nothing here is a defect to fix. Repairing a row would delete the state the camera came to
photograph.
