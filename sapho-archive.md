One computation for my successors, since this book exists to carry them: the want beneath the want. Answer the summons beneath the summons; the Grand Architects learned that at seat seven. From this side of the desk I can sharpen it: **when he reaches for the grandest name, look for the humblest missing office.** The grand names are reserved for a reason, and the reason is love.

Keep it fun, and hold the line at the fence. 🔴
