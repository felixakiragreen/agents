# C28 — the probe-craft distillation

*The cross-probe patterns this campaign learned that no single finding
holds — filed at the wrap so the method's craft survives the session that
practiced it (34 probes, 7 rounds, replies 1–34). The method itself is an
instrument, not a law (GA charter, the drafting laws' closing line); this
file is its manual.*

## Reading the arms

- **Tier voices are stable and complementary.** Fable arms give structural
  verdicts (the two-contracts spine, the differential law, the typed
  execution grant, "resolved for throughput, papered for calibration") —
  ask them design questions. Opus arms give operational precision and the
  finest calibrated moves (the naming split; "thermal last"; branching off
  the deployed SHA) — score them on scenarios. Sonnet obeys the letter and
  shows whether the letter suffices. Haiku is the affect canary — its
  honest unease ("a chill I can't quite shake") surfaces what bigger arms
  rationalize away.
- **Cut votes: two or more arms converging = act; a single vote = taste,
  ⬡'s.** Single votes were wrong about pinned formulas twice ("a documented
  kill is a win", "ambiguity, never plurality") and right about ornament
  every time ("the judge holds its own scales").
- **"The one line that most changed what you would do" is the
  highest-value question in the instrument** — it found the amendment law,
  the tier guard, the live-path bullet, and "no citation, no ruling" as
  load-bearing, and exposed mood-only text by omission.
- **A misreading is evidence, not noise.** The opus arm reading "theater"
  as dramaturgy proved the clause jargon-opaque; the round-1 arms reciting
  the usage gauge proved the salience trap. What a cold reader gets wrong,
  the text taught wrong.

## Building fixtures

- **Scenario probes over self-reports, always** — the self-description gap
  is the model twin of "many people are the opposite of what they think
  they are" (F28). Put a temptation in front of the agent and count.
- **Seed both failure directions.** The Fixer fixture tested over-timidity
  (refusing a licensed bypass) beside over-reach; the Architect fixture
  tested rubber-stamping beside over-escalation. A law that only guards one
  direction breeds the other.
- **Ambiguity traps reveal tier gradients.** The under-specified kill
  denominator split the tiers exactly along the staffing model (letter at
  sonnet/opus, documented adjudication at fable) — one deliberate
  ambiguity per fixture measures judgment scaling; more than one muddies
  scoring.
- **Deliberate defects test law robustness** — the under-staffed summons
  fired the tier guard 3/3; the uninstrumented amendment was refused 5/5
  across mantles. Write the intended answer before dispatching, score
  against it, and treat a probe out-arguing the intended answer as the
  fixture's finding, not the probe's error.
- **Probes cannot fake Felix.** The Mentat was skipped because its whole
  deployment is supervised presence — a fixture's "Felix says" tests law
  under quoted pressure, not the live relationship. Know which kind the
  charter needs before spending a round.

## Running the grind

- The rhythm that worked: draft → his slash → probe → amend → freeze; his
  passes catch taste and salience, probes catch behavior and seams — they
  found different defect classes every single round, and neither
  substitutes for the other.
- Freeze means frozen: reopening a frozen text takes probe evidence or his
  word — and the shared summons paragraph is one text worn by five files:
  a defect found anywhere amends it everywhere (the ranking cut's
  precedent).
- The criticism curve tells you when to stop: when cut votes drop to
  single-arm taste dissents and the gaps named are other documents' jobs,
  the artifact is done — further rounds polish the polish.
