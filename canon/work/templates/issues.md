# Issues — the incident inbox (D53)

Field reports and fold candidates land here mid-work — Felix's hand, or a session's
at his word. The Architect sweeps at every review sitting: each entry is ruled —
folded into the docs, cut as a board row, or rejected — then deleted; the ledger line
records rejections, and git keeps the bytes (entries are committed before they are
drained). A swept inbox is empty.
